What I do

  • Campaigns from scratch

    Branding, flyers, social media and email blasts planned and built from a blank page to a published campaign.

  • Graphic design

    Flyers, pull-out banners, magazine ads and social posts laid out for print and for the screen.

  • Video and voice-overs

    Coordinating shots, editing promotional videos and recording the narration for training tutorials.

  • Email marketing

    Email blasts and mass client updates, written, designed and sent from one tool to the right list.

  • Online visibility

    Search engine optimization, a steady presence across platforms and YouTube know-how, including monetization. Trained through two Google courses: Foundations of Marketing, and Attract and Engage Customers.

  • Three languages

    Fluent in English, Spanish and Portuguese, so a message can reach customers in the language they read.

The apps I work in

Most of my layout work happens in Adobe. InDesign carries flyers and multi-page booklets. Illustrator handles logos, vector artwork and signage. Photoshop edits and finishes the photos used in ads and social posts. When a campaign has to move fast, I build graphics and email blasts in Canva, then send client updates through Mailchimp. For video, I edit in Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro. In Audition, I record and clean up voice-overs. I publish to YouTube with its search and monetization tools in mind. The finished work goes out on Instagram and LinkedIn.
