The apps I work in

Most of my layout work happens in Adobe. InDesign carries flyers and multi-page booklets, Illustrator handles logos, vector artwork and signage, and Photoshop edits and finishes the photos used in ads and social posts. When a campaign has to move fast I build graphics and email blasts in Canva, and I send client updates through Mailchimp. For video I edit in Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro, record and clean up voice-overs in Audition, and publish to YouTube with its search and monetization tools in mind. The finished work goes out on Instagram and LinkedIn.
