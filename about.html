Background

I am originally from Venezuela. I have lived in 4 different countries: Venezuela, Colombia, Brazil and Canada. I speak three languages fluently: Spanish, English and Portuguese.

I have always been drawn to computers, technology and the progress of the industry. When I was around 14, I started making my own videos and posting them on YouTube. Some of them reached around 2.6 million views.

That response surprised me and made me even more invested in the field. In high school I took graphic design courses. I then studied Computer Science at Laurentian University, with a specialization in game design, where I got to know technology even better.

Today I have a broad set of skills that suit jobs beyond technology. I have held roles in sales, customer service, recruiting and project management, among others. My background and my ability to adapt have given me a skill set that works in any environment.
